# Hyperframes Composition Brief: SmartRide

## Objective
Create a short launch-style brag video for SmartRide, a dynamic ride-sharing and carpooling platform.

## Output

- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 20 seconds

## Source Material

- Project root: `D:\Web_Dev\WEB_Project\Ride-Sharing-project\Development-of-a-Dynamic-Ride-Sharing-and-Carpooling-Platform_September_Batch-3_2025`
- Primary files read: `client/src/components/LandingPage.jsx`, `PassengerDashboard.jsx`, `DriverDashboard.jsx`, `PaymentModal.jsx`, `client/src/index.css`, `README.md`
- Product name: SmartRide
- Tagline / strongest claim: "Share Your Journey, Save Money"
- Key UI or visual moment to recreate: the passenger search-to-booking flow, then a confirmed booking in the driver dashboard.

  - Same direction. Better ride.

Use fictional routes, people, and amounts only. Do not use source data, actual user identities, or any personal data.

## Creative Direction

- Tone preset: app-store
- Creative direction: clean, warm urban-mobility product film
- Interpretation: smooth, readable feature-card motion with warm yellow accents; practical and assured rather than flashy.
- Angle: one shared direction becomes a complete booking flow—matching, confidence, and live progress for both passenger and driver.
- Hook: route pins join beneath "Same direction. Better ride."
- Outro / punchline: SmartRide — "Share your journey. Save money."
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Unrelated visual redesign

## Storyboard

Use the storyboard in `brag-output/brag-plan.md` as the creative contract.

4. Built for both sides — 4s — passenger tracking and driver booking confirmation.
5. SmartRide — 4s — product mark, tagline, and calm feature row.
