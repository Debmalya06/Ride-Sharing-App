# Brag Plan: SmartRide

## What is this app?
SmartRide is a dynamic ride-sharing and carpooling platform where passengers discover route-matched rides and drivers post, manage, and earn from trips.

## The angle
The city is already moving. SmartRide turns one direction into a shared journey: find a verified driver, reserve a seat, track the trip, and split the cost. The film treats the quick passenger flow as the hero, while letting the driver dashboard prove there is a real two-sided marketplace underneath.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Same direction. Better ride." arrives above a moving route line that joins two location pins.

## Key moments (the middle)

- A passenger searches a Bengaluru-to-Koramangala route and matched rides appear one by one.
- A booking card confirms a seat, price, verified driver, and secure payment.
- A live route progresses while the driver dashboard receives the confirmed booking.

## Outro / punchline
SmartRide. "Share your journey. Save money."

## User flow worth showing
Choose passenger → search an available route → select a verified ride and book a seat → confirm payment and follow the trip; the driver receives and manages the booking.

## Tone

- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: clean, warm urban-mobility product film
- Interpretation: Smooth feature-card pacing, confident readable copy, and subtle route-inspired motion make the service feel practical and trustworthy.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: #f8fafc with warm yellow-to-orange hero treatment
- Accent: #eab308 / #f59e0b (SmartRide yellow)
- Text: #111827 / #333333
- Display font: Inter
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the warm yellow card language paired with route pins, booking states, and the dashboard's route rows.

## Share copy (draft)
SmartRide turns a shared direction into a safer, simpler, more affordable trip—match a route, reserve a seat, and ride together.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm, clean product bed with light interface accents
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 1 (upbeat, app-store energy)
- Music treatment: fade in under the route-line hook at a restrained 0.35 volume; lift slightly for the confirmation; fade under the final mark.
- Music cue guidance: detect at composition time via `hyperframes beats`; reserve strong beats near 4.0s, 12.0s, and 17.0s; use beat-grid windows for ride-card arrivals only when copy can hold.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; route glow and yellow confirmation halo may breathe with the music.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: route pin taps, sequential result cards, confirmation check, final logo.
- Restraint rule: no aggressive impacts or dense clicks; the product remains calm and trustworthy.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Same direction — 3s
Warm city-grid background with two yellow map pins connecting by a glowing route line. Large settled copy: "Same direction. Better ride."
Sequential/interaction: yes — the origin pin lands, then the destination pin; the line draws between them and holds.
Audio intent: a light, optimistic opening.
Audio-coupled idea: two restrained pin taps and a soft route-draw accent.
Music: upbeat bed begins quietly.
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Find a ride — 4.5s
Recreate the passenger search view with fictional locations: Indiranagar → Koramangala. A cursor presses "Find a Ride"; three clean results slide in one by one, each showing a verified-driver check, departure time, available seats, and price.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor click, then three route cards arrive at readable intervals and remain visible together.
Audio intent: controlled momentum.
Audio-coupled idea: click followed by light card-drop accents on the first and final result.
Music: steady, clean.
Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Reserve with confidence — 4.5s
One selected ride expands into a booking card: "1 seat reserved", "Verified driver", and "Secure payment" appear alongside a yellow confirmation check. The copy settles before the check holds.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated selection, then confirmation elements reveal in order.
Audio intent: a small, satisfying assurance moment.
Audio-coupled idea: selection click and one soft success bell on the completed check.
Music: warm lift.
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Built for both sides — 4s
Split composition: a passenger route moves along a simple live map on the left while a driver dashboard booking row on the right turns from PENDING to CONFIRMED. Small header: "Smart matching. Real-time tracking."
Sequential/interaction: yes — route dot moves, then booking badge changes to CONFIRMED.
Audio intent: quietly capable.
Audio-coupled idea: a single soft status-change accent.
Music: steady forward motion.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — SmartRide — 4s
Full-frame cream field, yellow route-line loop, and the product name. Settled tagline: "Share your journey. Save money." A final row quietly lists "Verified drivers · Flexible booking · Secure payments".
Sequential/interaction: none — logo and tagline enter cleanly, then hold.
Audio intent: polished close with room to land.
Audio-coupled idea: one gentle logo chime; music fades down.
Music: final uplift then fade.
Transition mood: hold to end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, clean, urban.
**Audio summary:** a friendly business-music bed supports a route-to-confirmation story, with only a few precise interface sounds.
